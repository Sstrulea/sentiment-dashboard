How the score is computed

Each indicator: the surprise (Actual − Forecast) compared with its last 12 surprises. Far beyond its usual size → ±2, above average → ±1, normal → 0. Rows without a forecast, or too old, do not enter.

Currency score. Macro = the mean of the growth, inflation and labour category averages, each divided by that currency’s usual spread; Carry = its policy rate (latest central-bank decision) minus the 8-currency mean; COT = speculative positioning. Score = 51% Macro + 34% Carry + 15% COT, each in its own σ units (a missing block is left out, the others rescaled).

Pairs. (base − quote) × 2.5; the US Dollar row uses the DXY positioning. Indices: 67% Macro + 33% Rates (the 3-month change of the home 2Y and US real 10Y yields; rising yields only erase a positive macro). Metals: 67% Rates (the 3-month change of the US 2Y yield) + 33% US Macro.

Score and bias: the raw score divided by that instrument’s usual size (its RMS over 3 years) gives z, shown as a Score from −10 to +10 — 0–2 Neutral, 3–5 Bullish/Bearish, 6–10 Very (about 55% of weeks are Neutral, 10% Very). Strength uses the macro part only.

N = how many indicators actually entered. A small N = less confidence.

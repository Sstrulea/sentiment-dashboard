Currency Strength

A currency's score = the mean over the 7 pairs on Economic it belongs to of 2.5 × (Macro base − Macro quote) (+ as base, − as quote): the macro part only — no carry, no COT. The 8 scores sum to 0. The bias compares the score with that currency's usual size (its RMS over 3 years): z = score / RMS, shown as a Score −10..+10 (0–2 Neutral, 3–5 Bullish/Bearish, 6–10 Very). Position (arc + %) = 50 + 5 × the Score before truncation. Click a card or table row for the per-indicator drilldown.

How the score is computed

FX pairs (scoring v4). COT positioning only. Each currency’s COT cell = Level (speculative net positioning against its 6-month and 3-year range, contrarian, −3..+3) + Flow (the 4-week change in net positioning, ±1), capped at ±4. Pair = 2.5 × (cell base − cell quote) / 1.77; the USD leg is 0 and the US Dollar row uses the DXY contract. The score changes once a week, after the Friday COT report.

Score and bias: the raw score divided by that pair’s usual size (its RMS over the last 156 COT weeks) gives z, shown as a Score from −10 to +10 — 0–2 Neutral, 3–5 Bullish/Bearish, 6–10 Very; the cut points are the 55th and 90th percentiles of |z| over the last 53 weeks, recomputed every week.

Indices: 67% Macro + 33% Rates (the 3-month change of the home 2Y and US real 10Y yields; rising yields only erase a positive macro). Metals: 67% Rates (the 3-month change of the US 2Y yield) + 33% US Macro. Each macro indicator: the surprise (Actual − Forecast) compared with its last 12 surprises. Strength uses the macro part only and does not enter the FX score.

N = how many indicators actually entered. A small N = less confidence.

Divergence = strongest − weakest score. A small spread = no exploitable macro divergence. N (the currency's indicators) is informative: each currency's Macro averages the categories it has.

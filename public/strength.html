Currency Strength

A currency's score = the mean of the fundamental scores of the 7 pairs on Economic it belongs to (+ as base, − as quote), without COT and without trend. It is in pair units, so the bias uses the same thresholds as the pairs, and the 8 scores sum to 0. Monetary enters a pair as the 2Y spread, only when both currencies have a fresh 2Y yield; the card shows each currency's own 2Y for information. Position (arc + %) = 50 + score × K. Click a card or table row for the per-indicator drilldown.

How the score is computed

We compare the surprise (Actual − Forecast) with the last 12 surprises of the same indicator. Far beyond its usual size → ±2, above average → ±1, normal → 0.

Scores are averaged within each category, then across categories → index. Rows without a forecast, or too old, do not enter the mean.

N = how many indicators actually entered. A small N = less confidence.

Divergence = strongest − weakest score. A small spread = no exploitable macro divergence. N (the currency's indicators) is informative: each pair compares only the categories both currencies have.
